Policy Context

A national platform for tourism policy and investment

Bangladesh has immense potential to become a leading tourism destination in South Asia, with its cultural heritage, diverse landscapes, archaeological treasures, rivers, biodiversity and vibrant communities. Tourism connects hospitality, transport, aviation, agriculture, handicrafts, culture and local entrepreneurship.

BTDS brings together policymakers, development partners, industry leaders, investors, academics, cultural professionals and community representatives to discuss, formulate and recommend strategic actions for the sustainable development of Bangladesh’s tourism sector.

Our Purpose

Objectives & Expected Outcomes

General objective: To establish a national platform for policy dialogue, strategic collaboration and investment promotion aimed at accelerating sustainable tourism development in Bangladesh.

Specific objectives

  1. Review the current status, challenges and opportunities of the tourism sector.
  2. Facilitate evidence-based policy discussions among government, private sector, academia and development partners.
  3. Promote tourism investment opportunities and destination development initiatives.
  4. Explore sustainable, community-based, eco, heritage and cultural tourism.
  5. Strengthen collaboration among stakeholders nationally and internationally.
  6. Develop actionable policy recommendations for the Government of Bangladesh.
  7. Position tourism as a priority sector for jobs, skills, entrepreneurship and rural transformation.
  8. Promote digital transformation and innovation in the industry.
  9. Strengthen inter-ministerial coordination for tourism development.
  10. Integrate tourism, culture, heritage and creative industries.

Expected outcomes

  • National consensus on key tourism development priorities.
  • Policy recommendations submitted to the Government of Bangladesh.
  • Priority tourism investment opportunities identified.
  • Stronger public-private-development partner collaboration.
  • Enhanced stakeholder engagement in sustainable tourism.
  • A framework integrating tourism, culture, heritage and the creative economy.
  • Greater recognition of community-based tourism and rural entrepreneurship.
  • Publication of the BTDS Declaration.
  • Greater awareness of tourism’s contribution to GDP, employment, exports and rural development.
Summit Outputs

Three Strategic Deliverables

BTDS aims to be a high-level national policy and investment summit, not simply a conference.

I

Bangladesh Tourism Development Declaration 2027

A long-term national vision and strategic framework for sustainable, inclusive and investment-driven tourism.

II

Tourism Investment Opportunities Book – Top Projects

Projects across destination infrastructure, hotels and resorts, eco-tourism, river cruises, heritage and community-based tourism.

III

Tourism-Related Policy Reform White Paper

Actionable recommendations for the Government, Parliament and development partners.
